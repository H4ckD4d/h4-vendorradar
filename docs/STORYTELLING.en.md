# H4 VendorRadar — Red Team thinking, defensive engineering

**Created and technically directed by Chris Cruz — @h4ckd4d.**

Red Team thinking asks what might fail, which trust boundaries matter, and whether safeguards can be tested. That curiosity inspires H4 VendorRadar. Vendor inventory and transparent review of evidence and document expiry.

We welcome responsible contributors worldwide: engineers, researchers, defenders, students and security teams. We support lawful work that protects human life and public safety, **without claiming endorsement or affiliation with any special-forces or government agency**.

**Mission:** make security measurable, useful and accessible. This is an engineering MVP, not a certified production service.
